Skip your test by driving past a van

AirCare’s RapidScreen units sit beside on-ramps and roads in white vans or small green boxes. They read the exhaust of cars driving past and match it to registration records.

2 You need two clean readings in the 14 months before your renewal.
Both must come at least 90 days before your renewal month. Renewing in July? Readings from May 1 last year to April 1 count.
Units run on weekdays between 5:30 AM and 7 PM, at sites that change every week. Bad weather can close a site early.

The app can’t see your readings. Waitlight is unofficial and not affiliated with AirCare Colorado.
